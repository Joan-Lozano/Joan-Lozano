# Joan Atinohaël Lozano Hidalgo

**React engineer based in El Salvador, open to remote roles across Latin America.**

Frontend developer at Grupo Campestre since 2023. I build and maintain the point-of-sale and administrative platforms behind 80+ branches: **500+ active users and 1,500+ transactions per day**, covering inventory, cash management, profit tracking and employee KPIs. I also help administer the branch infrastructure: each branch runs a local server and its own network, with services on Docker managed through Portainer.

## What I work with

- **Frontend:** React.js, Next.js, Vue.js, TypeScript, JavaScript, Tailwind CSS, Material UI, Ant Design
- **Backend and data:** Node.js, Strapi, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MySQL
- **CI/CD and infrastructure:** GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Docker, Portainer, Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Anthos)

## What I build at Grupo Campestre

Most of this work lives in the company's private repositories, so I describe it here by area:

- **Point of sale:** POS for restaurant branches, a POS kiosk app, a multiplatform POS client and the management back office behind them.
- **Warehouse and inventory:** warehouse management and handheld-device interfaces for stock control.
- **Employees and KPIs:** employee management and performance tracking.
- **Planning and operations:** material requirements planning (MRP) and project management tools.
- **Tax documents:** tooling for El Salvador's electronic tax documents (DTE).
- **Stores and web:** product display screens for stores and the corporate website.
